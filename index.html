    Meet Mandu, a small anteater drawn entirely in text characters. Mandu follows ant trails, and in AntRouter every trail leads a prompt to the right model. Tell Mandu which app you're in and it picks the right tier inside Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Words Mandu uses
    
          
    
            
    Trail
    What Mandu has learned about which model you prefer for a kind of prompt. Rerouting makes it stronger.
    
            
    Crumbs
    Points you earn by playing and spend in the shop, on second opinions or on forgetting a trail.
    
            
    Snatch
    Mandu's tongue grabbing an ant in the chosen model's color: the moment the pick is made.

    The top two models scored within a few points, or the prompt's keywords pointed two ways.

    Sniffing
    Gentle bob, swishy tail, wiggly snout, the odd tongue flick. Eyes follow your cursor.
    
          
    Following the trail
    Ants in the model's color march ahead, Mandu trots after them, and the trail sign flips to the destination.

    A head tilt and a question mark when you overrule it. Left alone, it curls its tail and dozes. Calm mode keeps it still.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Why an anteater
    
          
    It also helps that AntRouter is built at UC Irvine, where the anteater has been the mascot since the 1960s. Mandu is its own character, but it knows the cheer: type z-o-t anywhere on this page, or press the Zot! button, and it does a little blue-and-gold victory dance.
    
          
    Music: Carefree by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
    
          
    Real ants find the shortest path to food by laying scent trails, and computer scientists turned that into a routing method called ant colony optimization. AntRouter borrows the idea: every prompt leaves a trail, and the trails that lead to good answers get stronger. Mandu is the one who follows them. Its name comes from the tamandua, the anteater's smaller cousin, and its moods map onto the core loop: sniffing while you type, following the trail while AntRouter decides, happy when the answer arrives, puzzled when you steer.
